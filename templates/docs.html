Getting Started

Welcome to REDCON OFFENCE. Get started with our security platform in minutes.

REDCON OFFENCE provides two powerful security solutions: REDCON HAWK for AI-powered automated vulnerability scanning, and REDCON BLACKOPS for expert-led offensive security assessments. This guide will help you get started with both products.

Quick Start Steps

Follow these steps to maximize your security posture with REDCON OFFENCE:

1

Create Your Account

Sign up for a REDCON OFFENCE account to access both HAWK and BLACKOPS platforms. You'll receive 5 free scans with HAWK to get started. Visit the Client Portal to create your account.

2

Define Your Assets

Add the applications, APIs, or infrastructure you want to secure. Clearly defining your assets ensures comprehensive coverage during scanning and assessments. Include repository URLs, deployed endpoints, and any relevant documentation.

3

Run Your First Scan

Use REDCON HAWK to perform automated vulnerability scanning on your codebase. Our AI-powered scanner supports multiple languages including Solidity, JavaScript, Python, PHP, and Go. Get instant results with severity ratings and remediation guidance.

4

Request Expert Assessment

For comprehensive security coverage, request a BLACKOPS assessment. Our certified security professionals will conduct thorough penetration testing, identify business logic flaws, and provide detailed remediation guidance.

5

Remediate & Verify

Review findings, implement fixes, and run follow-up scans to verify remediation. Track your security posture over time with our dashboard and reporting tools.

6

Integrate Your Workflow

Connect REDCON with your existing tools like GitHub, GitLab, Jira, or Slack. Automate security checks in your CI/CD pipeline and receive real-time notifications about new vulnerabilities.
