## 💬 Sales Conversations

### Discovery Call Script (15 min)

```
OPENER (2 min):
"Thanks for taking the time, [Name]. Before I dive in, 
I'd love to learn more about what [Company] is working on 
from a security perspective."

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (8 min):

1. "Can you tell me about your current security testing 
   approach? Do you do pentests regularly?"

2. "When was your last pentest? How did that go?"

3. "What's driving the need to look at security testing 
   now? Compliance, upcoming audit, recent incident?"

4. "What does your tech stack look like? Web apps, APIs, 
   mobile, cloud infrastructure?"

5. "How many applications/assets would you want tested?"

6. "Who else is involved in this decision? CISO, 
   Engineering, Legal?"

7. "What's your timeline? When do you need this done by?"

8. "Do you have a budget in mind for this initiative?"

QUALIFICATION CHECK:
- Budget: > $10,000
- Authority: Decision maker or strong influencer
- Need: Compliance, audit, or security concern
- Timeline: Within 3 months

NEXT STEPS (5 min):
"Based on what you've shared, I think we could definitely 
help. Here's what I'd suggest as next steps:

1. I'll send you a brief proposal with pricing options
2. We schedule a technical deep-dive with your team
3. We can have you testing within [X] weeks

Does that work for you?"
```

### Objection Handling

**"We already have a pentest vendor"**
```
"Great - that shows you take security seriously. 
A lot of our clients actually came from other vendors.

What made you open to exploring alternatives?
(or)
If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing 
about your current approach, what would it be?"
```

**"We're too small / don't need pentesting yet"**
```
"I understand. Many founders think security can wait.

But here's what we've seen: companies your size are 
actually the most targeted because attackers know 
you have fewer defenses.

What if I could show you a quick assessment that 
identifies your biggest risks in under a week?"
```

**"We'll do bug bounty instead"**
```
"Bug bounty is great for continuous coverage, but 
here's the thing - it's not comprehensive. You're 
relying on researchers to find issues randomly.

A pentest gives you systematic coverage. Many of 
our clients do both: annual pentests + bug bounty 
for ongoing coverage.

Would that hybrid approach make sense for you?"
```

**"It's too expensive"**
```
"I hear you. Let me ask: what's the cost of a 
data breach for [Company]?

Beyond fines and remediation, there's customer trust, 
engineering time, and potential business loss.

Our clients typically see 10-50x ROI when we find 
critical vulnerabilities before attackers do.

What if we started with a smaller scope to prove 
value, then expanded?"
```

**"We need to think about it"**
```
"Totally understand. What specifically would you 
want to think through?

Is it budget, timing, or getting other stakeholders 
on board?

I'm happy to provide whatever you need to make 
the decision easier."
```
